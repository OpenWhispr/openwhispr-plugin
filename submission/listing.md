## Long description

Work with what you capture in OpenWhispr: notes, meeting transcripts, dictation history, your custom dictionary and snippets. Find what was said, recap a client call, and save the result back to your notes.

• Find answers in your notes and meeting recordings, with the note's name and date.
• Turn meetings into decisions, action items and open questions, then save a recap in the right folder.
• Review a week of captured work and recall past dictations.
• Add names and specialist terms to your dictionary, and create spoken shortcuts that expand into saved text.
• Check your usage and transcribe short audio clips supported by your account.

Six skills guide these workflows. Longer recordings can use the existing OpenWhispr command-line tool when the assistant has terminal access on your computer; otherwise, import the recording in the OpenWhispr desktop app and work with the saved note.

Sign in with your OpenWhispr account. The connection can read and change your personal cloud-backed notes, folders, dictionary and snippets when you ask. The skills ask before replacing or deleting existing content. Notes in team spaces and content kept only on your device are outside the remote connection's access. Cloud backup must be enabled for your captured content to be available. Content retrieved by your assistant becomes part of that conversation.
